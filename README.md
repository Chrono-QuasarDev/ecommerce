## 📖 Overview

**Bɔfoɔ Style Spectrum** is a Ghanaian custom merchandise and print-on-demand business based in **Accra, Ghana**. The business specializes in turning customers' personal ideas and designs into tangible, wearable, and decorative products.

The brand name draws from the Twi word *"Bɔfoɔ"* (meaning **hunter/marksman**), symbolizing precision and intentionality in every custom creation. Bɔfoɔ Style Spectrum positions itself as a one-stop destination for personalized fashion and lifestyle products, built for individuals who want to express themselves boldly and uniquely.
